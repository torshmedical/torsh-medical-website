## Keep as vector assets

The 33 navigation, interface and category symbols are intentionally SVG. They share a 64-unit geometry, adapt to light/dark surfaces, remain sharp at every pixel density and inherit accessible colour states. Replacing them with raster “HD icons” would reduce quality and consistency. The existing icon audit remains in `docs/ICON-AUDIT.md`.

## Do not fabricate yet

- **Product pages:** wait for verified model-level product photography or approved technical renders. Generating photorealistic product images now could imply released devices, dimensions or features that have not been established.
- **Clinical/procedure imagery:** wait for appropriately licensed and reviewed material; generic surgical imagery can introduce clinical inaccuracies.
- **Partner, certification and outcome marks:** require verified organizations, approvals or evidence before creation.
